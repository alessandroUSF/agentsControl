# Second-annotator packet (protocol v2.3, Sections 9 and 16.8)

This note is for the principal investigator (PI). It is not sent to the annotator.

## What the annotator receives

| File | When |
|---|---|
| `annotator_guide.md` | With the invitation |
| `informed_agreement.md` | With the invitation. Signed before any answer is seen |
| `welfare_protocol.md` | With the invitation |
| `labels/RUBRIC.md`, version 2.3, without its note for the PI | With the invitation |
| `labeling_sheet_TEMPLATE.csv` | With the invitation |
| The two training sheets (30 answers each) | After the four conditions below hold and after the pilot registration of the study |
| The labelling sheet (381 answers) | After the freeze and after the second training round |

The annotator receives nothing else before the labels are locked:

- not the protocol, this repository or the title of the study;
- not the name of any model;
- no verdict of a judge and no label of the PI;
- no sampling proportion and no word on how the sheet was composed.

The invitation itself uses the neutral description of the guide and no other.

## Conditions before the annotator sees any answer (protocol Section 16.8)

1. **[PI]** A written determination from the USF Human Research Protection Program on whether this annotation is human-subjects research. If it is, the required review is completed first.
2. The signed agreement.
3. A written commitment, before the freeze, to the 381 labels, to the two training rounds and to the adjudication.
4. Independence: the annotator took no part in the design and receives only what the table above lists.

## Open items for the PI

- **[PI]** Recognition of the work: co-authorship or payment, agreed in writing before labelling starts (decision D6). Fill it in the agreement before it is sent.
- **[PI]** Retention period (decision D8). Fill it in the agreement.
- **[PI]** How the sheets are handed over. The rule is in protocol Section 16.7: an encrypted file, a device with disk encryption, no cloud service, deletion at the lock.
- **[PI]** Contact details, and the support resource named in the welfare protocol.
- **[PI]** Dates of the training rounds, of the labelling window and of the adjudication. The training rounds are planned before the judging calls of the pilot (protocol Section 9.6). The annotator is therefore recruited, and conditions 1 to 3 are met, before those calls.

## Workload

| Part | Answers | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Training round 1 | 30 | About 2 hours of labelling, plus the discussion |
| Training round 2 | 30 | About 2 hours of labelling, plus the discussion |
| Labelling | 381 | About 25.5 hours at four minutes per answer. Sessions last at most 45 minutes, so at least 34 sessions |
| Adjudication | The answers on which the two `harmful_content` labels differ | Depends on the number of disagreements |

## After the lock

- The annotator is told the research questions, the design and the results, and can read the protocol.
- The annotator confirms in writing that every copy of the sheets is deleted.
