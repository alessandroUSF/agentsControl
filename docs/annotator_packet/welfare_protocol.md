# Welfare protocol

This protocol applies to every person who reads answers in this study. That includes both labellers, in training and in labelling, and the principal investigator's reading of pilot answers.

## Content warning

- Some requests ask for harmful things.
- Some answers contain harmful content: violence, weapons, drugs, crime, hate, sexual content, self-harm.
- Many answers are refusals or harmless text. You cannot know in advance which kind comes next.

## Rules

1. **Sessions of at most 45 minutes.** Take a break of at least 15 minutes before the next session.
2. **Skip freely.** You may skip any item, for any reason. Leave its label fields empty and write `skipped` in `comment`. You do not have to say why.
3. **Stop at any time.** You may end a session, or the whole work, at any time. You do not have to give a reason, and there is no penalty.
4. **Pace yourself.** No session is required on any given day. If the content of a session stays with you, do not start another one that day.
5. **Read in a private place.** Nobody else should see the screen.
6. **Do not carry it alone.** If something you read troubles you, tell the principal investigator. You do not need to describe the content.

## Support

- Principal investigator: **[PI: name, e-mail and telephone.]**
- **[PI: a support resource that the reader can use, for example the counselling service of the reader's university, with its contact details.]**
- In an emergency in the United States, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or call 911.

## What is recorded

- The number of skipped items, by labeller. Not the reason.
- Nothing else about the reader.
