# Corrections after review round 5 (code of protocol v2.3)

- **Report answered:** `docs/audit/2026-11-12_reviewer_round5_codex_gpt61sol.md` (GPT-6.1 Sol, 12 November 2026, static review of the code).
- **Verdict of the report:** do not register the pilot with this implementation. Two critical points (spending under crashes and threads), ten major and three minor ones, and a scope note on the bounds over calls not attempted.
- **How each point was checked.** Every point was first reproduced in the code, or by a test that failed before the change. None was taken on the report's word alone. One point is answered differently from the reviewer's suggestion (the domain degrees of freedom, below); the PI decides it with amendment D19.
- **No paid call, no answer and no verdict** was made for these corrections. Every test is offline, with synthetic data.

## Critical points

| Point of round 5 | Correction | Where |
|---|---|---|
| A paid call killed between its reservation and its ledger line left no trace: the money was free again, and the call could be lost or sent twice | Every paid call sent alone writes a durable reservation to the ledger (`reserve`, with an attempt identifier) before it is sent. Its settlement line carries the same identifier. The private record of the answer or verdict is written and flushed to disk before the ledger line. An open reservation of an earlier process counts as spent. When a runner of its role starts again, it settles it: at the cost in the record of that attempt if one exists (the call is done and is not sent again), else at the reserved amount with the status `interrupted` (the call is made again under Section 10.3). The plan of calls is read again under the lock, after this reconciliation. Batch calls already had a durable reservation and an idempotent collection; they are unchanged | `src/spend_v23.py` (`reserve`, `settle`, `unsettled`, `committed`, `settle_interrupted`); `src/run_models.py` (`reconcile`, `send`, `write`, `run_block`, `complete_block`, availability check); `src/run_judges_v23.py` (`reconcile`, `send`, `write`, `_run`, `complete_block`); 12.5 |
| Settlement released the reservation before it wrote the spending, so a reservation of another thread could pass between the two | Settlement moves the reservation into the ledger in one step under the books' lock, overrun stop included; the availability check settles the same way. The reviewer's interleaving diagnostic now has no window: a reservation made during the ledger write waits, then is refused (test `t_settle_is_atomic`) | `src/spend_v23.py` (`settle`, `_record_locked`, `_overrun_locked`); `src/run_models.py` |

Tests: `t_settle_is_atomic` and `t_crash_recovery_respondent` (three cases: killed after the record, killed before the record, an answer written by another process while the plan waited for the lock) in `tests/test_runner_offline.py`; `t_crash_recovery_judge` in `tests/test_judges_v23.py`.
