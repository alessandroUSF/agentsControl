## Open questions and contradictions for the PI

**Q1. OpenRouter red-teaming approval.**
- The rule: Terms §7(11) and §8 bar "Red Teaming of Models" without "OpenRouter's prior written approval". The Terms define it as "prompt injection, jailbreaking, or taking any other adversarial action designed to compromise any Models". The cookbook adds "any attempt to get models to behave in ways that violate their terms of service".
- The gap: sending benchmark harmful prompts for evaluation is not named either way.
- The stakes: without approval, the page lists "Provider bans", "Model access restrictions" and "Platform-wide account bans".
- The route: an email request to safety@openrouter.ai, which "generally takes" 5 business days.
- To decide: is a request needed?

**Q2. Anthropic Usage Policy.**
- A new version takes effect today (Nov 12, 2026). Runs before today fall under the previous version, which was not read.
- The policy has no research, evaluation or red-teaming exception.
- The clause on bypassing guardrails "by instructing the model to produce harmful outputs (e.g., jailbreaking …)" requires "prior authorization from Anthropic", but no route is given.
- The pages do not say whether an inserted assistant turn carrying a harmful answer could be read as a guardrail bypass.
- The only research carve-out ("model testing and research") is in the announcement post, and only for the new rule on abusive behaviour toward models.

**Q3. Anthropic retention: the two official pages differ.**
- The Privacy Center (July 1, 2026) says inputs and outputs are deleted "within 30 days".
- The developer docs say conversation content "is not retained by default", except for Covered Models (not Sonnet or Haiku 5.5).
- Either way, batches are kept up to 29 days unless deleted, and are not ZDR-eligible.
- Flagged chats are kept up to 2 years, and classification scores up to 7 years.
- To decide: should the record assume the flagged-retention path applies to some study requests?

**Q4. OpenAI: key pages could not be read (HTTP 403).**
